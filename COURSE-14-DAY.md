# Placement Sprint — 14-Day Course Outlines
**Brand:** Placement Sprint · placementsprint.in  
**Audience:** India CSE / BCA / BBA, sem 6–8  
**Price that this earns:** ₹4,999 Sprint · ₹7,999 Sprint+  
**Promise:** Recruiter-ready artifacts in 14 days — **not** a job offer / package guarantee.  
**Student load:** ≤90 min/day. No hour-long theory dumps.
**Naval tightens (locked):** Day 14 = realistic apps not forced 20 · Day 11 scorecard ≤24h always · Day 10 coach-assigned sheet only · no soft-skills day.

---

## How to run each day
| Block | Who | Time |
|-------|-----|------|
| Brief | Coach (WhatsApp / Loom ≤5 min) | 5 min |
| Do the work | Student | 45–75 min |
| Submit | Student → shared folder / WhatsApp | — |
| Review | Coach (async or live) | 10–20 min |

**Done rule:** Day is complete only when the day’s **Done criteria** are checked.

**Refuse to promise in any lesson:** placement, CTC/package, “guaranteed interview,” fake alumni.

---

## Day 1 — Role lock (not “open to anything”)

**Goal:** One target role + why + constraints. Kill spray-and-pray identity.

**Student work (≤75 min)**
1. Pick **one** role title you’d accept in the next 90 days (e.g. Junior SDE, Data Analyst Intern, Business Analyst, Frontend Intern).
2. Write 5 bullets: skills you already have that map to that role.
3. List constraints: city / WFH / stipend min / start date.
4. Fill **Role Brief v0** template (coach provides).

**Coach**
- Push back if role is vague (“software” / “anything in IT”).
- Confirm role matches degree stream (CSE vs BCA vs BBA).

**Deliverable:** Role Brief v0 (1 page).

**Done when:** Role title is specific enough that a stranger could Google 10 matching JD titles.

**Sprint+:** Same day — coach suggests 2 alternate role titles if first is too crowded for their skill level.

---

## Day 2 — Company map (10 targets)

**Goal:** 10 companies/roles that fit Day 1 brief. Quality > quantity.

**Student work (≤75 min)**
1. Find **10** concrete targets (campus drive OR off-campus posting OR LinkedIn job).
2. For each: company · role title · link · 3 keywords from JD · why you fit in one line.
3. Rank top 5 “apply first.”

**Coach**
- Kill fantasy targets (FAANG-only with zero projects).
- Keep 2–3 stretch + 7 realistic.

**Deliverable:** Company Map (table, 10 rows).

**Done when:** Every row has a live link + 3 JD keywords.

**Sprint+:** Coach marks which 5 will get Day 13–14 application-review support.

---

## Day 3 — Resume autopsy + outline

**Goal:** Destroy the novel. Build a one-page skeleton for the role.

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Paste current resume. Self-score with **Resume Scorecard** (clarity / impact / role-match / ATS / honesty) 1–5 each.
2. Cut everything that doesn’t serve Day 1 role.
3. Draft section order: Header · Education · Projects/Experience · Skills · (optional Certs if role-relevant).
4. List 3–5 projects/experiences that will get impact bullets tomorrow.

**Coach**
- Mark “cut / keep / rewrite” in red on their PDF or Google Doc.
- Ban: objective paragraphs, soft-skill walls, 3-page résumés.

**Deliverable:** Resume Outline + filled scorecard.

**Done when:** Outline fits one page in principle; ≤5 project/experience slots.

---

## Day 4 — Resume v1 (impact bullets)

**Goal:** Resume v1 — every bullet is action + context + result (or concrete output).

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Write bullets using: **Verb + what you built/did + tool/stack + result/output**.
2. Quantify where honest (users, % , time saved, lines only if meaningful). Never invent metrics.
3. Skills = stack for the role, not 40 buzzwords.
4. Export **Resume_v1.pdf** (one page).

**Coach**
- Rewrite 3 weakest bullets live or async.
- Check ATS: no tables/text boxes; standard section headers.

**Deliverable:** Resume_v1.pdf

**Done when:** Coach can skim and state the candidate’s role fit in ≤60 seconds.

---

## Day 5 — Resume v2 (final for Sprint)

**Goal:** One revision pass → **final** Sprint resume (unless Sprint+ later tweaks).

**Student work (≤60 min)**
1. Apply coach Day 4 notes.
2. Peer skim optional: send to 1 friend — “what job am I going for?”
3. Export **Resume_FINAL.pdf** + plain-text version for portals.

**Coach**
- Final approve or one more tight pass (max).
- Save before/after for pilot proof (permission).

**Deliverable:** Resume_FINAL.pdf

**Done when:** Stranger skim test passes (coach or peer names the target role correctly).

**Sprint+:** Optional later JD-tailored variant for top 3 companies (Day 13).

---

## Day 6 — LinkedIn foundation

**Goal:** Profile stops saying only “Student at…”

**Student work (≤75 min)**
1. Photo rules: clear face, plain background, no group selfies.
2. Headline formula: `{Role aim} | {1–2 proof skills/stack} | {Degree/College short}`.
3. About: 4–6 short lines — who you are, what you’re building toward, proof, CTA (open to roles / internship).
4. Custom URL if possible.

**Coach**
- Rewrite headline if generic.
- Check About for fluff.

**Deliverable:** LinkedIn draft (screenshot or doc with sections).

**Done when:** Headline states a role + proof skill.

---

## Day 7 — LinkedIn match + featured

**Goal:** LinkedIn matches resume; Featured shows one project.

**Student work (≤75 min)**
1. Experience/Education aligned with Resume_FINAL (same titles, dates, story).
2. Featured: 1 project (GitHub / Notion / Drive demo / Canva case).
3. Skills: pin top 5 for the role.
4. Turn on Open to Work (students: visible to recruiters only if preferred).

**Coach**
- Checklist approve: photo · headline · about · featured · skills · consistency with resume.

**Deliverable:** Live LinkedIn URL + LinkedIn Checklist signed off.

**Done when:** Coach opens URL and sees role-fit in 10 seconds.

---

## Day 8 — STAR bank (stories 1–4)

**Goal:** Interview machine starts — 4 scored stories.

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Write 4 STAR stories (Situation, Task, Action, Result) from projects/college/internship.
2. Themes: teamwork conflict · hard problem · deadline · leadership/initiative.
3. Keep each story ≤90 seconds spoken.

**Coach**
- Mark weak Results; force specificity.
- Kill childhood / irrelevant stories.

**Deliverable:** STAR Bank partial (4/8).

**Done when:** Each story has a clear Result line.

---

## Day 9 — STAR bank (stories 5–8) + “Tell me about yourself”

**Goal:** Complete 8 STARs + 45-second intro script.

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Add 4 more STARs: failure/learn · disagreement · achievement · “why this role.”
2. Write **Tell me about yourself** in 45–60 seconds: present (student + stack) · past proof · future (role) · close.
3. Record once on phone (private). Listen back.

**Coach**
- Edit intro for filler (“basically,” “like”).
- Confirm 8 STARs cover HR + behavioral.

**Deliverable:** STAR Bank complete (8) + Intro script + optional voice note.

**Done when:** Student can say intro without reading.

---

## Day 10 — Role drill (tech or case)

**Goal:** One **coach-assigned** drill matched to stream — not a LeetCode/YouTube binge.

**Student work (≤90 min)**
- Work **only** the coach-assigned drill sheet for their role. No self-directed problem binge.
- **CSE / BCA sheet (coach picks one track):** 4–6 problems (arrays/hash/strings) **or** explain one project end-to-end (architecture, tradeoffs). Time-box.
- **BBA sheet:** One mini-case (market entry / metrics / customer problem) + structured answer.

**Coach**
- Assign the sheet (do not say “go practice DSA”).
- 15-min async review of answers OR Loom feedback.

**Deliverable:** Coach-assigned drill sheet completed + notes.

**Done when:** Coach scores that sheet ≥ “pass with notes” (not blank).

**Sprint+:** Deeper drill pack (extra **assigned** set); review before Mock 1.

**Hard rule:** No soft-skills / mindset day exists in this course — do not add one.

---

## Day 11 — Mock Interview 1 + scorecard

**Goal:** First real pressure. Hurt on purpose. Written scorecard.

**Format:** 30–40 min live (Zoom/Meet) or structured async video answers if timezone forces it. Prefer live.

**Sections:** Intro · 2 behavioral STARs · 1 role drill question · “Any questions for us?”

**Student work**
- Be on time, Resume_FINAL + LinkedIn open.
- After: rewrite 2 weakest answers same day (≤30 min).

**Coach**
- Use **Mock Scorecard**: clarity · structure · depth · honesty · role-fit (1–5).
- Send written scorecard **≤24h for every Sprint student** (same as Sprint+). 14-day product dies if feedback lands after Day 12 mock.

**Deliverable:** Mock 1 recording (optional) + Mock 1 Scorecard + 2 rewritten answers.

**Done when:** Scorecard sent and student acknowledged 2 fixes.

---

## Day 12 — Mock Interview 2 + scorecard

**Goal:** Second mock shows improvement. Exit behavioral readiness.

**Format:** Same length; harder follow-ups on Day 11 weak spots. Mix HR + role.

**Student work**
- Rehearse fixed answers 20 min before.
- Post-mock: update STAR bank with better Result lines.

**Coach**
- Compare Mock 1 vs 2 scores.
- Explicit “what still fails in a real panel.”

**Deliverable:** Mock 2 Scorecard + updated STAR bank.

**Done when:** At least 2 score dimensions improved vs Mock 1 **or** clear written plan for remaining gaps.

**Sprint+ track pick (kickoff reminder):** either **+2 extra mocks across days 11–14** OR **1 extra mock + 1 project/app review** — must be scheduled, not theoretical.

---

## Day 13 — Applications system (batch 1)

**Goal:** Targeted apps, not 80 silent applies.

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Open **Apps Tracker** (20 slots).
2. Submit **10** applications from Company Map (tailor 3 bullets or keywords per JD — honest only).
3. Log: date · portal · resume version · status · follow-up date.

**Coach**
- Spot-check 2 apps for JD match.
- **Sprint+:** written feedback on up to 5 apps **before** submit (async).

**Deliverable:** Tracker with 10 logged apps.

**Done when:** 10 real submits logged with links/screenshots if possible.

---

## Day 14 — Applications batch 2 + exit review

**Goal:** Finish targeted apps from Company Map (cap 20) · follow-up scripts · exit scorecard. **Do not force spam applies.**

**Student work (≤90 min)**
1. Submit remaining apps from Company Map until every **realistic** target is logged (hard cap **20**). If fewer open drives/postings exist, stop — do not invent junk applies.
2. Copy **follow-up scripts** (LinkedIn + email) into tracker.
3. Self-fill **Exit Scorecard**: resume · LinkedIn · mocks · apps · confidence (1–5) + “what’s still missing.”

**Coach**
- 20–30 min exit review (call or long voice note).
- If apps < 20: **coach-sign** the tracker (“realistic max for this week”) so Done isn’t a fake 20.
- Strong / missing / next 14 days solo plan.
- Remind: we sold artifacts + system, not a job.

**Deliverable:** Tracker filled for every realistic Company Map target (cap 20) · follow-up scripts · Exit Scorecard · coach exit note.

**Done when:** Tracker complete for realistic targets (coach-signed if < 20) **and** exit review archived (student + coach).

**Sprint+:** Start **7-day post-sprint async Q&A** window (text). No extra live calls unless sold separately.
